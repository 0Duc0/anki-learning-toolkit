# Deck Progress

Use the **Config** button for the graphical settings window, or open
**Tools → Learning Toolkit → Deck Progress…**.

On **Counter**, choose the decks and whether to count cards, notes, or both.
Use **Card states** to count answered items, all items, or a mix of new,
learning, relearning, young review, and mature review cards. A note counts
once when any selected card from that note matches. Labels are customizable.
Enable **Require every card of a note to match** to check siblings across all
decks. For fully learned notes, select **Young review** and **Mature review**
and turn off **Include suspended cards** to ignore suspended siblings.
**Only a. stages in counter decks** can restrict a selection to Stage Shifter's
a. decks.

On **Progress**, turn on **Show selected total** or **Show progress bar and
percentage**. The counter count comes from your counter decks. The goal total
includes the counter decks and cards in additional goal decks that match
**Goal deck states**. Shared cards or notes count once.

The default **Answered at least once** filter uses Anki's current answer count.
Counters are independent, local to this computer, and saved per profile.

This add-on displays counts only. It does not move or change cards.
