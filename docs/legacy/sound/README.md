# Sound Volume (Claude) 1.0

One volume for all card audio, adjustable while you study.

- Tools → Sound Volume…: volume 0–200 %, mute, playback speed 0.5–2×, optional
  "even out loud and quiet recordings" (each recording is measured once and played at the same loudness), Replay card audio, Reset.
- Shortcuts everywhere in Anki: ⌘⌥↑ louder, ⌘⌥↓ quieter, ⌘⌥M mute (Ctrl+Alt on
  Windows/Linux). A short tooltip shows the new volume.
- Applied to Anki's audio player (mpv) right before every sound and immediately when
  you change something, so even a playing sound changes.
- Display/audio only: nothing in your collection changes. Text-to-speech is not affected.
